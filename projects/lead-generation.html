PROBLEM

Manual lead research can become slow and inconsistent.

Businesses often spend too much time finding prospects, organizing information, and deciding which leads are worth prioritizing.

OBJECTIVE

Create a more organized lead qualification process.

The goal of this demo is to structure prospect research, collect useful lead data, and support qualification with clear criteria.

TOOLS

Research + Spreadsheet + AI Assistance

Web Research, Google Sheets, structured qualification criteria, and AI-assisted analysis.

WORKFLOW

From research to a cleaner final lead list.

Research → Data Collection → Google Sheets → AI Qualification → Final Lead List

QUALIFICATION LOGIC

How Leads Are Evaluated

Each lead can be reviewed using a simple set of business-fit criteria before being prioritized for outreach.

01

Industry Fit

Does the business match the target industries defined for the campaign?

02

Business Relevance

Is the prospect likely to benefit from the service or solution being offered?

03

Data Quality

Is enough useful and reliable information available to evaluate the prospect?

04

Priority Score

The collected criteria are combined to support a simple lead-priority score.
